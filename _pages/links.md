## Recommended reading

- [*Enigmas of Chance*](https://books.google.com/books/about/Enigmas_of_Chance.html?id=1ZWl39UJWycC), a biography of Mark Kac

- [*Infinitesimal*](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374534998), on the conflict between the church and mathematicians over infinity

- [*The Man Who Knew Infinity*](https://www.amazon.com/Man-Who-Knew-Infinity-Ramanujan/dp/1476763496), a biography of Srinivasa Ramanujan

- [*A Mathematician's Apology*](https://www.math.ualberta.ca/mss/misc/A%20Mathematician%27s%20Apology.pdf), G. H. Hardy's view of a mathematician's life

## Lutsko family science

My father, [Jim Lutsko](https://lutsko.com), is a physicist at the Université libre de Bruxelles studying crystallization, nucleation, and density functional theory.

My brother, [Nick Lutsko](https://nicklutsko.github.io/), is a climate scientist at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography studying climate sensitivity and the representation of complex atmospheric phenomena in idealized models. He discusses visualizing climate change in [this exchange with artist Michael Wang](https://www.artnews.com/art-in-america/interviews/visualizing-climate-change-michael-wang-nick-lutsko-1202681918/).
